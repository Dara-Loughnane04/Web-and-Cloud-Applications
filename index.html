2026 was a very successful year for the Offaly Senior Hurling Team. They competed in Division 1A of the Allianz National Hurling League.
Unfortunately they lost all six of their games against Kilkenny, Tipperary, Waterford, Galway, Limerick and Cork.
Offaly gave some very good performances and came up short in all these games. A lot of players on the panel were injured and missed a lot of the matches.
In the Leinster Senior Hurling Championship, Offaly did very well in it. They played five matches.
The matches were against Dublin, Galway, Kilkenny, Wexford and Kildare.
In the first round, Offaly played Dublin in Glenisk O'Connor Park in Tullamore. The match finished as a draw Offaly 4-22 2-28 Dublin.
In the second round, Offaly played Galway in Pearse Stadium in Salthill. Galway won the match 2-26 to 1-18.
In the third round, Offaly played Kilkenny in Glenisk O'Connor Park in Tullamore. The match finished as a draw Offaly 0-24 1-21 Kilkenny.
In the forth round, Offaly played Wexford in Glenisk O'Connor Park in Tullamore. Offaly won the match 2-21 to 2-15.
In the fifth round, Offaly played Kildare in Cedral St Conleth's Park in Newbridge. Offaly won the match 1-29 to 015.
After the fifth round game against Kildare, Offaly finished third place in the Leinster Round Robin table.
They secured their place in the All-Ireland Senior Hurling Championship Quater-Finals.
Offaly played Cork in their All-Ireland Quater Final. Cork won 6-25 to 2-11.
The match was played in Semple Stadium in Thurles, County Tipperary.
